How It Works

How building with us works

From the first call to the keys.

1. A quick phone chat

We talk about your needs, wants and budget range.

2. Site visit

We visit the site and plan how to achieve your needs and wants within budget.

3. Design with your architect

We help you assess your lot, collaborate with your architect and attend design meetings on team, budget and suggestions.

4. Proposal and build

We review the proposal with you, stake the house for a site meeting, then build and coordinate every phase.

Our Story

Our story

McCreary Homes has been incorporated since May 1988. LJ McCreary has been building custom luxury homes in Arizona since then, after designing and building custom mesquite furniture. McCreary Homes was one of the first certified custom home builders with SAHBA and is a member of NAHB.
